--- title: "BedreFlyt" excerpt: "BedreFlyt is a Digital Twin for Resource Planning in Healthcare.
" collection: portfolio --- The BedreFlyt digital twin integrates several formal techniques into a tool chain for prescriptive analysis. Our digital twin combines formalized domain knowledge (using knowledge graphs) about patient treatments and hospital wards, an actor-based executable formal model (with ABS) to explore strategies for streams of incoming patients with associated treatments, and an optimizer (via Z3) to perform the actual bed bay allocation. The orchestration language SMOL connects the different components. The BedreFlyt digital twin is currently being developed at the University of Oslo (UiO) with collaboration with dScience, SINTEF and joint work with other universities. The project can be found on GitHub.
