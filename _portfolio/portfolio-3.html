OsloFjord DT is a digital twin platform for the Oslo fjord. It allows for real-time monitoring and simulation of fish species, enabling researchers and stakeholders to better understand and manage the fjord's ecosystem. The platform integrates data from various sources, including sensors, buoy drifters, and historical records, to create a comprehensive digital representation of the fjord.

Manual and documentation are available in the GitHub repository.
